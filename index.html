How it works

What you get

  • 01Drop-in for Playwright & Puppeteer.One-line endpoint change. Your selectors, waits and assertions are untouched.
  • 02Stable sessions for agents.The browser stays alive between connects — in-memory SPA state and open tabs, not just cookies. Survives our redeploys too.
  • 03Structured /extract.URL plus a JSON Schema in, validated JSON out.
  • 04Live session viewer.Watch a run in real time. Share a signed link that carries no API key.
  • 05MCP server.Browser tools for Claude, Cursor and anything else that speaks MCP. No integration code.
  • 06CAPTCHA solving included.An allowance on every paid plan, not a per-solve markup.
  • 07Per-second billing.Floored at 10 seconds. No unit maths, no rounding a 3-second job up to 30.
  • 08EU-hosted, no lock-in on your code.Falkenstein. You connect over standard CDP, so your Playwright and Puppeteer scripts stay portable — there's no proprietary SDK to rewrite if you ever move.

Every plan includes CAPTCHA solving, stealth, stable sessions and the live viewer — nothing here is gated to a higher tier. Browser time is billed per second, floored at 10 seconds, which works out at about $0.14–0.22 an effective browser-hour. Most services bill in 30-second blocks and round up, so a 3-second job costs you 10 seconds here and half a minute there. A stable session left parked keeps billing until it idles out — the dashboard lists what's running so you can stop it. Proxies stay yours to buy at wholesale; we don't mark them up.
